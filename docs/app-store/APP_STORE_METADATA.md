## Description

PharmAtlas is a private medication organization companion for individuals and caregivers. Build reusable schedules and regimens, document administration history, track inventory, receive native iOS reminders, and review monitoring information in one place.

Flexible scheduling supports daily, weekly, every-other-day, interval, PRN, and user-specified routines. Individual doses can be retimed without changing the rest of a medication schedule, or future doses can be rescheduled together when the routine changes.

PharmAtlas also includes searchable linked-medication workflows with And, Or, and Followed by relationships; configurable monitoring targets and critical-result guidance; inventory alerts; printable reports; medication adherence summaries; caregiver notes; monitoring reminders; and backup and restore. Optional QR pairing supports direct same-network synchronization. Advanced users may configure their own encrypted HTTPS relay so changes can wait while the desktop is offline; no developer-operated PharmAtlas account or relay is included.

Data is stored on the device. Information leaves the device only when the user deliberately exports, shares, emails, backs up, stores files in a synchronized location, enables companion sync, or configures a remote relay. Remote relay payloads are encrypted on-device. Reminders are generated locally by iOS and do not use email.

PharmAtlas is intended for personal organization and caregiver documentation. It does not provide medical advice, prescribe treatment, replace a pharmacist or clinician, or provide emergency services. Always verify medication instructions and changes with a licensed healthcare professional.

## App Privacy Draft

Re-evaluate App Privacy answers with Apple before submission because the build can transmit user-encrypted ciphertext to a user-configured third-party relay. Do not rely on encryption alone when deciding whether a category is considered collected. Disclose camera access for QR pairing, local-network access for initial trust pairing/direct sync, and the optional user-configured remote relay in review notes.

The app does not contain advertising, analytics, third-party tracking, or a developer-operated account service. `PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy` declares no developer data collection and no tracking. If a developer-operated relay is added later, update both the manifest and App Store Connect privacy answers before uploading that build.

## Suggested Age Rating Answers

The app contains no mature content, gambling, social networking, or user-generated public content. Health information is entered privately by the user. Confirm every answer in App Store Connect against the final binary.
