# PharmAtlas Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026

PharmAtlas is a personal medication organization application. The app stores medication schedules, member profiles, administration history, inventory, monitoring entries, notes, labels, and settings locally on the user's device.

## Data Collection

The developer does not operate an account service, analytics service, advertising service, or remote database for PharmAtlas and does not receive the information entered into the app.

## User-Initiated Sharing

Information leaves the device only when the user deliberately exports a file, prints, shares, emails, creates a backup, or stores the app or its data in a third-party synchronized location. Those actions are governed by the privacy practices of the destination service selected by the user.

## Data Retention And Deletion

Data remains on the device until the user edits or deletes it, restores a different backup, clears the app's local data, or removes the app and its stored data. Users should retain backups before deleting the app if they want to preserve records.

## Security

Users are responsible for securing their device, device account, backups, exported documents, email account, and synchronized storage. PharmAtlas is not a substitute for a clinical record system.

## Children

PharmAtlas is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through a developer-operated service.

## Contact

Privacy questions can be submitted at `https://github.com/DanielAU11/pharm-atlas/issues`.

This policy must be reviewed whenever the app adds analytics, accounts, cloud synchronization, crash reporting, or any other service that transmits data to a developer or third party.
