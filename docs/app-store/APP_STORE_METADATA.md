## Description

PharmAtlas is a private medication organization companion for individuals and caregivers. Build reusable schedules and regimens, document administration history, track inventory, receive native iOS reminders, and review monitoring information in one place.

Flexible scheduling supports daily, weekly, every-other-day, interval, PRN, and user-specified routines. Individual doses can be retimed without changing the rest of an order, or future doses can be rescheduled together when the routine changes.

PharmAtlas also includes searchable linked-order workflows with And, Or, and Followed by relationships; configurable monitoring targets and critical-result guidance; inventory alerts; printable reports; medication adherence summaries; caregiver notes; monitoring reminders; and backup and restore. An optional QR-paired companion connection synchronizes changes directly with PharmAtlas Desktop while both apps are open on the same local network. The connection is encrypted and does not use a PharmAtlas cloud account.

Data is stored on the device. Information leaves the device only when the user deliberately exports, shares, emails, backs up, stores files in a synchronized location, or enables local companion sync. Reminders are generated locally by iOS and do not use email.

PharmAtlas is intended for personal organization and caregiver documentation. It does not provide medical advice, prescribe treatment, replace a pharmacist or clinician, or provide emergency services. Always verify medication instructions and changes with a licensed healthcare professional.

## App Privacy Draft

Select **Data Not Collected** only after confirming that the submitted build contains no analytics, advertising, developer-operated account service, remote database, or third-party SDK that receives user data. User-initiated exports, backups, shares, and direct local-network device sync remain under the user's control. Disclose camera access for QR pairing and local-network access in review notes even though the developer does not collect the transferred data.

The app contains no mature content, gambling, social networking, or user-generated public content. Health information is entered privately by the user. Confirm every answer in App Store Connect against the final binary.
